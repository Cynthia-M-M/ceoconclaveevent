Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CEO Conclave 2025

CEO Conclave is an exclusive gathering of business leaders, innovators, and investors. It provides a platform for networking, knowledge sharing, and exploring business opportunities across various sectors.

The event is open to C-level executives, business owners, investors, and senior decision-makers. We welcome both local and international delegates from various industries.

  • • Access to all keynote sessions and panel discussions
  • • Gala dinner and networking events
  • • Conference materials and documentation
  • • Business matching opportunities
  • • Post-event access to presentations

We offer various sponsorship tiers including Platinum, Gold, and Silver packages. Each tier comes with different benefits such as brand visibility, speaking opportunities, and VIP access passes.

Business formal attire is required for all sessions. Black tie is recommended for the gala dinner.

Refunds are available up to 30 days before the event with a 15% administrative fee. Alternatively, you can transfer your registration to another eligible attendee.
